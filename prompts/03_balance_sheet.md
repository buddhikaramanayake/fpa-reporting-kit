# Section prompt 3 — Balance Sheet

Build the Balance Sheet view.

- Use closing balances at the **last month of the selection**. Ignore the Period control and say so on screen.
- Comparison: Budget at the same date, or the same month of the prior year.
- Groups: Assets, Liabilities, Equity, with totals. Show a balance check (Assets − Liabilities − Equity = 0).
- Working-capital panel: current ratio, DSO, inventory days, net cash/(debt). State the day-count method.
- Commentary must name the evidence needed (ageing, purchase orders, cash bridge) before a cause is stated.
