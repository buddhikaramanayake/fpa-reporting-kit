# Section prompt 1 — Dashboard

Build the Dashboard view.

- Five KPI tiles for the selection: Revenue, Gross margin %, EBITDA, Net income, Closing cash (at the last month of the selection).
- Each tile shows the comparison as $ and %, plus a Favourable / Unfavourable tag based on **profit impact** (not the raw sign). Gross margin change is shown in basis points.
- Chart 1: monthly revenue for the selected year — Actual (closed months), Forecast (open months, dashed), Budget.
- Chart 2: monthly EBITDA, selected scenario vs comparison.
- "What moved profit": the three lines with the largest absolute profit impact, written with the commentary template.
- No dual axes. Every chart has a legend and hover tooltips.
