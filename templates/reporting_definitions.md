# Reporting definitions

Confirm these before the build. Change them to match your accounting policies.

| Term | Definition used |
|---|---|
| Period flows | Revenue and expenses are summed across the months in the selection. |
| Closing balances | Balance sheet items are taken at the last month of the selection. Never summed. |
| Gross Profit | Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold − Fulfillment & Freight |
| EBITDA | Gross Profit − operating expenses (excludes D&A, interest, tax) |
| Margin | Profit total ÷ Revenue total for the selection |
| Variance | Selected − Comparison |
| Profit impact | Revenue lines: variance. Cost lines: −variance. Positive = favourable. |
| Latest Estimate | Actuals for closed months + Forecast for open months |
| Prior Year | Actuals for the same months one year earlier |
| Materiality | \|impact\| ≥ $50K, or \|impact\| ≥ $25K and \|variance\| ≥ 5% |
| DSO / inventory days | Closing balance ÷ closing-month flow × 30 (directional) |

Sample-data simplifications (replace with your policies): tax = 25% of monthly profit before tax; cash is the balancing figure on the balance sheet.
