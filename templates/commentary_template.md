# Commentary template

One bullet per material line. Four parts, in this order:

1. **Difference** — {Line} is {$ amount} ({%}) {above/below} {comparison} for {period}; {favourable/unfavourable} to profit by {$}.
2. **Evidence** — what the data shows: when the gap opened, whether it persists, whether one month carries it, related lines that moved together.
3. **Next step** — the specific analysis or decision needed (not "monitor").
4. **Owner** — a named role or person.

Rules
- A balance or a variance is not a cause. If the cause is not in the data, write the evidence needed ("carrier invoices for Jul–Sep") instead of guessing.
- Use the same numbers as the tables.
- Say "one-off" only when one month carries most of the variance and the owner has confirmed it.

Example
> **Fulfillment & Freight** is $69K (+11.1%) above Budget YTD Oct-2026 — unfavourable to profit by $69K. The gap opens in July and persists, so it looks like a run-rate change. Next step: pull carrier invoices for Jul–Sep and split rate, surcharges and volume. Owner: Logistics Manager.
