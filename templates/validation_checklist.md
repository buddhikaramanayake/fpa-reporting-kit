# Validation checklist

Run before the pack is circulated. A failed check stops circulation until resolved.

- [ ] Balance sheet balances for every month and scenario
- [ ] Retained earnings closing = opening + net income (− dividends)
- [ ] Latest Estimate = closed actuals + forecast for open months; closed months unchanged
- [ ] Income statement subtotals are calculated, and Revenue − all costs = Net income
- [ ] YTD revenue and net income agree with the ledger / reported totals
- [ ] Every account mapped (or listed as unmapped)
- [ ] Missing data shows "—", not zero
- [ ] Commentary numbers agree with the tables
- [ ] Known gaps and untested items listed in the file
